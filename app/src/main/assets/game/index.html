Firefly
Choir

Flash your lantern in rhythm with the fireflies until the whole meadow lights up as one.

How to play

  1. Each firefly glows brighter just before it flashes.

  2. Tap to flash your lantern. Fireflies close by flash sooner and fly toward your light.

  3. Flash together with a group and its neighbours join in. Get every firefly flashing at once before dawn.

Your lantern holds five flashes and refills slowly. Big groups refill it faster.
