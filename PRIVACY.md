# Privacy Policy — Firefly Choir

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

Firefly Choir is a free game by MhamzaM (FreeApps by Hamza).

- The app **does not collect, store remotely, or share any personal data.** It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- The app **has no internet permission**, so it cannot send anything off your device.
- Your best score and settings (sound on/off) are saved only on your phone, and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- The only permission is **vibration**, used for a short buzz during play.

The game is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/MhamzaM/firefly-choir/issues
